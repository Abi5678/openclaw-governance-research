## Background

The current paper already positions SARC, authorization overlays, ROMA-style orchestration, path-based governance, telemetry, and agent benchmarks.

That makes it a strong contrast case for our claim: OpenClaw-Govern is not just runtime enforcement, but the deterministic composition of multiple runtime controls.
The current review cycle is focused on evaluation consistency, so this increment should stay scoped to related-work positioning and avoid reopening benchmark numbers.
