# OpenClaw Research Increment — 2026-06-30

## Objective
Add a concrete audit-reconstruction / trace-reconstruction benchmark scenario so OpenClaw-Govern measures unified-trace completeness as a first-class composition property, not just final verdict accuracy.

## Background
OpenClaw-Govern’s thesis is about composition: SARC-style runtime constraints, authorization propagation, async controls, ROMA-style delegation, ordering semantics, conflict resolution, and unified traces.
The current benchmark already covers deterministic governance failures, but the evaluation story still leans heavily on verdict correctness.
The paper’s trace-tree section defines trace completeness as reconstructing the full decision path, which means the benchmark should explicitly score evidence presence and short-circuit markers.
`docs/evaluation-plan.md` already names an `audit reconstruction` scenario, so the missing step is to turn that into a concrete, ground-truthed benchmark case and export field.
ROMA is relevant because its recursive hierarchy makes delegation chains traceable, but ROMA alone does not arbitrate governance conflicts across modules.
OPA decision logs and OpenTelemetry trace conventions give a practical precedent for reconstructable, correlated decision records.
XACML combining algorithms are useful precedent for deterministic arbitration, but they do not address heterogeneous runtime-module trace completeness.

## Implemented increment
- Added an `audit_reconstruction` scenario to `experiments/composition_benchmark.py` and `experiments/composition_benchmark_real_adapters.py`.
- Exported a per-row `trace_completeness` field in both benchmark CSVs.
- Preserved explicit `skipped` trace markers for short-circuited modules in the audit-reconstruction path.
- Synchronized `paper/07_evaluation.md`, `paper/main.md`, and `docs/evaluation-plan.md` with the 9-scenario benchmark framing.

## Acceptance criteria for builder
1. The benchmark contains a dedicated audit/trace reconstruction case with a fixed expected verdict.
2. Benchmark output exports a trace-completeness or trace-coverage field that can distinguish full reconstruction from fragmented logs.
3. The real-adapter benchmark preserves trace markers for short-circuited modules, not just executed ones.
4. `paper/07_evaluation.md` reports the new scenario and treats trace completeness as a primary metric.
5. The updated scenario aligns with the trace-tree definition of reconstructable decision paths.

## Out of scope
- No new runtime dependencies.
- No attempt to broaden the benchmark into unrelated safety benchmarks.
- No commits or pushes.
